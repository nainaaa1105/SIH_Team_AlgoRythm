How to Navigate
Map

Search a state, city or region, or click any coloured dot for that fire's full detail. Scroll or use the + / − controls to zoom — zooming in close switches from the 3D globe to a flat map automatically.

My State / All India

A state account starts scoped to its own state; switch to All India to see the whole country, or back to My State any time. An administrator always sees All India.

Control Panel (left)

Fire Classification checkboxes decide what's drawn on the map — unticking a class hides every fire of it, including WUI Threat and Crown Fire.

Sensor Data picks which acquisition days and instruments (MODIS/VIIRS) are included. Filters and Map View hold the rest — confidence, minimum FRP, state and basemap options.

Live Summary (right)

The total fire count and Classification Breakdown always match what the map is currently showing. WUI Critical and Crown Fires isolate just those safety-critical detections.

Fire Detail

Click a fire to see its classification, sensor readings, model reasoning (SHAP) and spread prediction, plus a live suppression-resource estimate. From here you can zoom to it, download a full PDF report, or send a test emergency SMS.

Bottom Bar

Shows the live latitude/longitude under your cursor and the total fire count for whatever's currently filtered.
