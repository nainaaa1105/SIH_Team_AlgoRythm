FireSight — Operator Guide

Navigating the globe

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to tilt. Below the subcontinent altitude the camera locks to India and returns automatically if you pan away.

Reading a detection

  • Hover the globe for live latitude, longitude and the administrative area under the cursor.
  • Hover a fire marker for a summary card.
  • Click a marker for the full record — detection, location and geometry.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • / focus search   L layers   F filters
  • A alerts   H home   R reset
  • +/− zoom   Esc close panels
